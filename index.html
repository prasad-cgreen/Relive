So, what's going on?

Har situation ka ek way out hai. Pick the one that sounds like you.

"2–3 EMIs miss ho gayi hain. Late fees badhti ja rahi hai, and my score keeps dropping."

The fastest fix: clear the overdue EMIs so the account is regular again, then foreclose it. We negotiate the penal interest and late fees down with your lender, and the loan gets reported as "Closed".

"I settled a loan two years ago. Ab har bank reject kar deta hai."

A "Settled" tag can often be upgraded. We talk to the lender about paying the shortfall in exchange for a "Closed" status and an NOC, then make sure the bureau record is updated.

"Two app loans, two cards, one personal loan. Every week some EMI is due."

Debt Consolidation pays them all off with one loan from a partner lender. One EMI on one date makes it far easier to pay on time, which is what your score needs most.

"I'm paying 18% on my personal loan when my friend got 12%."

If your repayment record is clean, a Balance Transfer can move your loan to a lender with a lower rate. We compare offers and show you the real saving after fees.

Questions? We've got you.

Will checking my score on Relive lower it?

No. Checking your own report is a "soft" enquiry and doesn't affect your score. Only applications for new credit, where a lender pulls your report, count as hard enquiries.

How long before my score improves?

It depends on what's on your report. Clearing overdues and closing accounts usually shows up within one or two reporting cycles, since lenders now report to the bureaus every 15 days. Building a strong score takes several months of clean repayments on top of that.

Can you remove a "Settled" or "Written-off" tag?

Accurate history can't simply be deleted. But a lender may agree to update a settled account to "Closed" if you pay the amount they waived. We negotiate that for you and follow up until the bureau reflects it.
